Building on the ideas that powered Google’s early scalability ( read [[How google handled BIG data]] ), [Doug Cutting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doug_Cutting) and [Mike Cafarella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Cafarella) brought those concepts to the open-source world with the creation of the **Hadoop Distributed File System (HDFS)**. Their work democratized large-scale data processing, making it possible for anyone — not just tech giants — to store and analyze massive datasets across clusters of ordinary machines. HDFS became the backbone of the big data revolution, inspiring an entire ecosystem of distributed computing systems.
